1. **High Dimensionality**: The input space in NLP is complex and large, making it harder to process with RLHF 
algorithms designed for simpler tasks.

longer training times or increased resource usage.  
3. **Task Complexity**: RLHF requires handling a lot of information, making the problem more intricate for tasks 
like language modeling or sequence generation.  
4. **Data Preprocessing**: Poorly prepared data can lead to errors, which may impact the model's performance.  
5. **Integration with Existing Systems**: Integrating RLHF into existing frameworks or systems may require 
modifications, which could be a barrier.
